Task Prioritisation - Pickle Jar

The Pickle Jar Theory

Imagine your available working time as an empty pickle jar. If you fill it with Sand (social media, micro-distractions) or Pebbles (routine admin tasks) first, you won't leave room for the big Rocks (core deliverables & high-impact goals). Prioritise by placing your critical Rocks in the jar first, filling around them with Pebbles, and allowing Sand only in remaining idle spaces.
